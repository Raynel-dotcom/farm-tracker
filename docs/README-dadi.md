# Farm Tracker — Plain-Language Guide for Dadi

Hello Dadi! This guide will show you how to open and use your **Farm & Rental Tracker** application. It is designed to help you keep track of all your rental payments, matooke banana sales, coffee sales, and farm expenses in one simple place.

---

## 1. How to Open the Tracker

1.  **Locate the File**: Open the **`farm tracker`** folder on your computer.
2.  **Open the App**: Double-click on the **`farm-tracker.html`** file inside the `frontend` folder.
3.  **Start Using**: It will open automatically in your web browser (like Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge). You do not need any special internet setup to view it, but being connected to the internet allows it to save your data to the cloud.

---

## 2. Navigating the App (The Sidebar)

On the left side of the screen, you will see a green column (the sidebar) with five buttons:
*   📊 **Overview**: The homepage. It shows your summary totals, a graph of your performance, and a button to save your files to Excel.
*   🏠 **Rental**: Where you enter rental income and see who owes you balance.
*   🍌 **Matooke**: Where you enter matooke banana sales.
*   ☕ **Coffee**: Where you enter coffee sales.
*   💸 **Expenses**: Where you enter money spent on farm inputs or repair work.

---

## 3. How to Enter Your Data

### 🏠 Recording a Rent Payment (Rental Page)
When a tenant pays rent, go to the **Rental** page and fill in:
1.  **Date Received**: Choose the day they paid you.
2.  **Property / Tenant**: Enter their name and property (e.g. *Main House - Mr. Okello*).
3.  **Month Covered**: Select the month they are paying for.
4.  **Agreed Rent**: The full monthly rent they are supposed to pay (e.g. *500,000*).
5.  **Amount Paid So Far**: The actual amount they handed you.
    *   *Note*: If they paid in full, enter the same amount. If they paid part, enter the lower amount. The system will automatically show the outstanding balance!
6.  Click **Add rental payment**.

### 🍌 Recording Crops Sales (Matooke & Coffee Pages)
When you sell crops, go to the **Matooke** or **Coffee** page:
1.  **Date**: Select the sale date.
2.  **Quantity**: Enter the number of bunches (for Matooke) or kilograms (for Coffee) sold.
3.  **Price**: Enter the price per bunch or price per kg.
4.  **Notes**: You can write who bought it (e.g. *Sold to Kampala Buyer*).
5.  Click **Add sale**.

### 💸 Recording Farm Costs (Expenses Page)
When you spend money on the farm or rentals:
1.  **Date**: Choose the day you spent the money.
2.  **Category**: What it was for (e.g. *Fertiliser, Labour, Repairs, Land Tax*).
3.  **Amount**: Enter how much money was spent.
4.  **Notes**: Add details (e.g. *Bought 3 bags of urea*).
5.  Click **Add expense**.

---

## 4. Deleting an Entry
If you make a mistake and want to delete an entry, simply click the red **Remove** button next to that specific line in the table.

---

## 5. Saving Your Records to Excel

You can download all your records to open them in Microsoft Excel:
1.  Go to the **Overview** page.
2.  Click the button at the top right that says **📥 Export everything (Excel)**.
3.  This downloads a file called `farm-rental-tracker.xlsx` to your computer containing separate sheets for your Rentals, Matooke, Coffee, and Expenses!
